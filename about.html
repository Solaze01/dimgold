About Me

I'm Dada Oladimeji (DimGold) - a Brand & Product Designer who believes design is more than pixels and palettes; it's about creating experiences people can trust, love, and remember. With nearly a decade of design practice, I've helped startups and businesses in fintech, AI, logistics, and edtech tell their stories through products that feel as good as they look.

But more than a job, design is my way of exploring transitions - turning ideas into systems, and problems into stories worth telling.

Why Design?

I design because it's how I see the world. Every interface is a story waiting to be told. Every product is an ecosystem waiting to be understood. I'm drawn to transformation stories, whether in people, brands, or systems. That's why my work always seeks to merge strategy with soul.

Beyond Design

When I'm not designing, you'll probably find me:

  • Deep in a game of chess, sharpening strategy and patience.
  • Hosting or scripting for Transition with DimGold, my podcast/book for creatives navigating their own journeys.
  • Mentoring designers who remind me why I started.
  • Or just quietly journaling, reflecting on life, faith, and growth.

I thrive on curiosity, creativity, and conversations that matter.

My Ideology

At the core, I believe design is more than pixels and layouts, it's purpose.

My work flows from a conviction that creativity is a gift from God, meant to bring clarity, order, and transformation to the world.

My philosophy is simple:

  • Design should serve destiny, not just aesthetics.
  • Stories should glorify God, not just impress men.
  • Work should build people, not just products.

Every project I take on is an opportunity to reflect light, unlock potential, and create impact that outlives trends.
